Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Slide Studio (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and application.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and basic profile information provided by your sign-in provider (for example Google).
  • Usage data: prompts and instructions you send for slide generation, project/slide content stored for your workspace, feature usage, and approximate credit/token consumption.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (or other processors). We do not store full card numbers; we may receive transaction status and customer email from the processor.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service (including AI slide generation).
  • To authenticate users, manage credits, and process purchases.
  • To prevent abuse, debug errors, and ensure security.
  • To communicate service-related messages when necessary.

3. AI processing

Prompts and related content may be sent to third-party AI providers (such as large language model APIs) solely to generate the requested slides. Do not submit sensitive personal data you are not authorized to process.

4. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

We may share limited data with:

  • Infrastructure and AI providers needed to run the Service;
  • Payment processors (e.g. Lemon Squeezy) for checkout and receipts;
  • Authorities when required by law.

5. Retention

We retain account and project data while your account is active and as needed for legitimate business, legal, or security purposes. You may request deletion of account-related data subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

  • You can stop using the Service at any time.
  • You may request access or deletion of personal data by contacting support through the in-app channel.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated Policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the in-app feedback/support option or contact us via the email linked to your account provider.
