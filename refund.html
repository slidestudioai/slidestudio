Refund Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

All sales are final. Purchases of credits, subscriptions, or other digital products on Slide Studio are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or as expressly stated below.

1. Why sales are final

Slide Studio delivers digital goods: AI slide generation and related server compute. When you request generation, third-party AI APIs and our infrastructure consume paid resources immediately. Because of this digital nature, we cannot “return” unused compute in the same way as a physical product.

2. Credits and subscriptions

  • Credit packs and paid balances are non-refundable once the purchase is completed.
  • Subscription fees (if offered) are non-refundable for the current billing period after the period has started, except where mandatory consumer law requires otherwise.

3. Technical errors and failed deductions

If a technical error causes credits to be deducted without a corresponding successful operation, or if a failed generation incorrectly consumes credits, we will work to resolve the issue.

  • Contact support through the in-app feedback channel and describe the time of the error, your account email, and what you expected to happen.
  • After verification, we will replenish lost credits to your account rather than issuing a cash refund, unless the payment provider or law requires a different remedy.

4. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback. Unwarranted chargebacks may result in suspension of the account while the dispute is reviewed.

5. Payment provider

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (or another listed processor). Their terms may also apply to billing and tax handling.

6. Contact

For refund-related support (including technical credit issues), use the in-app support/feedback option with your account email and purchase details.
